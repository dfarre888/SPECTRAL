# SPECTRAL — do not look generated

The dark ops floor already has a voice (A3DM store: zinc, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, orange as warning not brand paint). The light theme failed when it became Apple-glass + Inter + frosted pills. That is the 2026 default.

Read `.cursor/skills/no-ai-slop/TELLS.md` before restyling. Then apply these product constraints.

## Forbidden on SPECTRAL chrome

- `backdrop-filter` frost / iOS materials on briefing paper
- Inter (or "system-ui") as the personality face
- Purple-to-blue or orange-to-pink gradients
- Hero pills, three icon cards, fade-up-on-scroll
- Soft grey body copy on cream (`text-zinc-400` on `#f4f4f5`)
- Rounded-xl everything
- Em-dashes in UI copy
- Decorative motion on commander controls

## Required

- **Display:** Space Grotesk. **Data:** JetBrains Mono. Body can stay Inter only as a workhorse, not a look.
- Light theme is **paper / print**, not iOS Settings. Opaque surfaces. Hairline rules. Ink near-black.
- Orange `#F97316` / `#ea580c` is caution and primary action, not a wash.
- Classification banner stays amber. Do not theme it into pastels.
- Globe / Cesium stays night. HUD over the globe stays white.
- Jane's density: every label earns its place. No empty luxury spacing.

## Skills to pair

- `no-ai-slop` — tell catalog
- `emil-design-eng` — craft and restraint (not bounce)
- `frontend-design` — pick a direction and commit; do not average

Do not apply landing-page "taste" variance dials to Map Intel. This is a staff tool.
